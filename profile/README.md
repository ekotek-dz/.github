## What we're building

Kitchens can't reduce what they don't measure. EKOTEK is building a smart bin that records every disposal:

1. **Weigh** — a scale in the bin detects each disposal and its weight.
2. **Recognise** — a camera and AI models identify the food, and flag items that don't belong (plastic, metal, glass, paper).
3. **Report** — kitchens see what they waste, how much, and when, so they can buy, cook and portion better.

The system has three parts:

| Part | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Hardware & connectivity | Camera, integrated scale, edge device, network |
| AI models | Food segmentation and classification, contamination detection, running on the edge |
| Dashboard & data | Event ingestion, storage, analytics and reports for kitchens |
